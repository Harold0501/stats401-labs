Temporal Network Questions

01

How does overall network connectivity change across the 60 days?

Connectivity fluctuates across the first 40 days, with active links increasing and decreasing from day to day. After day 40, the network becomes noticeably more connected. All twelve companies are active on several late-period days.

02

Which companies become more central or commercially active over time?

BlueRiver and Fusion are prominent early. Fusion dominates days 21-40 through new ties to Harbor, Lumen, and Juniper. In the final phase, Ion and Cedar become the largest hubs.

03

Are there periods when the network consists of relatively separate clusters?

Separate clusters are especially visible on days 1, 3, 7, 9, 11, 13, 17, and 19. On these days, active relationships form several disconnected groups rather than one connected network.

04

Which commercial relationships appear, disappear, or become stronger over time?

BlueRiver-Delta and Cedar-Fusion disappear after day 20. Fusion gains three strong ties in days 21-40, then Cedar-Granite, Cedar-Keystone, and Ion-Fusion emerge for the final 20 days.

05

Does the network become more or less cross-regional over time?

Overall, the network becomes more cross-regional over time, with this pattern becoming more noticeable in the later days. More links gradually connect companies located in different regions.
