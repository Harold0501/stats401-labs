Design Description and Findings

Passages come from the PDF's own layout where PyMuPDF text blocks were assigned to the bookmark hierarchy (chapter, section, subsection) by locating each bookmark title at the start of a line, and blocks were merged only on layout or continuation evidence such as a lowercase continuation or a bulleted list after a colon. Page margins, footnotes, duplicates, tables, and their lead-in sentences were excluded by fixed rules, and passages longer than the model's 256-token window were split at sentence boundaries, so no text is silently truncated. The whole pipeline runs without manual editing.

Each passage was embedded with the normalized all-MiniLM-L6-v2 sentence transformer. UMAP projected those vectors to two dimensions using 15 neighbors, cosine distance, a 0.15 minimum distance, and random seed 401. K-means grouped the original high-dimensional vectors into nine clusters, and each cluster is named by its three most distinctive terms. Words that appear in many of its passages but comparatively few passages elsewhere. Nine clusters provide a readable resolution with enough topics to separate disciplines, rules, and institutional content, while remaining distinguishable as colors and as matrix columns.

In the semantic map, position encodes embedding similarity, color identifies the interpreted topic, and area represents passage length. Search section, and semantic filters retain context by muting rather than removing unmatched points, while zoom and pan support dense regions. Clicking a point anchors the full passage and its five cosine-nearest neighbors in a persistent panel. The matrix complements local neighborhoods with a structural summary. Rows are formal bulletin sections in document order, grouped by Part, columns are semantic topics, and cell darkness is the number of passages on a log scale. Selecting either a point or matrix cell highlights the corresponding mark in the other view, making mismatches between where a passage appears and what it discusses directly traceable.

The overview charts establish corpus scale and a lexical baseline before the model-based views. Muted marks preserve the global UMAP shape during filtering, and encoding counts rather than within-section shares keeps one-passage sections from appearing as dominant as the large ones. Tooltips support quick scanning, while the persistent detail panel keeps source context visible long enough to compare neighboring passages and return to their original pages.
